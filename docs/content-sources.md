## Product notes

Pack size is taken from the public tables and, where visible, the photograph. Several tables on the medicines page are inconsistent, so every product asks for pack confirmation.

- **G Appet** — listed for loss of appetite and bloat. Pack photograph also reads indigestion, anorexia, bloat and acidosis. Cattle illustration. Silhouettes include other livestock, including a horse. Not listed under Horses.
- **G Liv** — one entry says liver support for dogs; the pack photograph used here reads Anorexia, shows a dog, and 15 ml; another listing is general veterinary. A file named as G Liv dog medicine actually shows **G Milet** packaging. That file was not used.
- **G Uddar** — listed as an udder tissue formulation. Pack photograph reads atrophied / small udder and shows cattle.
- **G Eliksir** — listed as a metabolic stabiliser. Pack photograph shows a cow. Other silhouettes are not treated as confirmed species.
- **G Petworm** — listed for deworming in dogs. Pack reads Deworming and 15 ml. Listing names roundworms and hookworms.
- **G Dermi** — pack photograph says skin problems and shows livestock. Another entry describes a dog formulation for skin and ticks. Both categories are shown, with a confirmation note.

No horse-specific product is invented. The Horses filter is an empty state with a team enquiry.
